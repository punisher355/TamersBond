# **Chapter 10: Growth & Experience**

There are no levels in Tamer's Bond. Characters grow by spending EXP. Every purchase is a tradeoff.

## **Earning EXP**

| **Event** | **EXP Reward** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short or light session | 75–150 | Mostly roleplay, travel, or downtime |
| Standard session | 200–400 | A mix of combat, exploration, and story |
| Heavy or climactic session | 400–600 | Boss fights, major revelations, high stakes |
| Story milestone | 500–1,000 | Defeating a major villain, finding a crest, arc resolution |
| Personal character moment | 100–300 | Awarded individually for standout roleplay or growth |

| **How EXP Pools Work** Tamers and Digimon each have their own separate EXP pool. They do not share one pool. Both pools always contain the same total EXP earned. Example: The GM awards 350 EXP. The Tamer's pool gains 350. The Digimon's pool ALSO gains 350 independently. Spending EXP on the Tamer does NOT reduce the Digimon's pool and vice versa. |
| --- |

## **Campaign Pacing**

| **Campaign Stage** | **Approx. Total EXP** | **Expected State** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session 1 (creation) | 1,500 | Stats at 1–3, a few skills at 2–3, clearly defined but fragile |
| Season 1 midpoint | 8,000–10,000 | Primary stat pushing rank 4–5, core skills at 3–4 |
| Season 1 end | 15,000–20,000 | Primary stat at 5–6, secondary stats 3–4, first classes purchased |
| Season 2 end | 35,000–45,000 | Primary stat pushing rank 8, skills nearing rank 5 |
| Full completion | ~70,000+ | All stats and skills maxed, all desired classes purchased |
