# Chapter 7: Who Is Your Digimon?

Your Digimon partner is a full character, not a sidekick to your Tamer. They have their own personality, their own history, their own Crest Stats, and their own EXP pool. Everything from here through Chapter 8 is about giving that character the same weight on paper that your Tamer already has.

## Choosing Your Starting Digimon

Work with your GM to pick your Digimon's starting Rookie form: a specific Digimon, like Agumon or Gabumon or Patamon, not an abstract archetype. That single choice is really all you need to make here. Everything downstream of it, Species Base in every Crest Stat (Chapter 8), Attribute, Element, and every Champion, Ultimate, and Mega form your partner can eventually reach, is already defined by that Digimon's own evolution line. You're not building a family tree from scratch; you're picking which existing one your partner belongs to, and the system handles the rest.

Don't pick a starting form on stats alone. Consider what kind of personality this Digimon has, why they work well with your particular Tamer, and what the shape of that relationship actually looks like day to day. A Digimon chosen purely for a strong Species Base plays very differently at the table than one chosen because their whole personality clicks with what your Tamer needs.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | What does your Tamer call them? |
| Starting Digimon (Rookie Form) | The specific Digimon you're starting as, chosen with your GM |
| Personality | Two or three words that sum them up |
| Want | What does this Digimon want, beyond just growing stronger? Chapter 1 has a table of common answers if you want a starting point. |
| Fear | What would actually shake them? |
| History | How did they and their Tamer first meet? |

## Their Own EXP Pool

> **Starting EXP Budget**
>
> Your Digimon partner starts with the same 1,500 EXP as your Tamer, spent entirely separately.
>
> From here on, the two pools stay linked without ever touching. Every time your Tamer earns EXP, your Digimon earns the exact same amount into their own pool at the same moment (Chapter 9 covers how EXP gets awarded). Spending EXP on your Tamer never reduces what your Digimon has to spend, and spending on your Digimon never reduces your Tamer's pool. The two of you are always working with matching totals, even though what you've each bought with that total can look completely different.

With who your Digimon is settled, Chapter 8 covers what they can actually do: the four layers that build their Crest Stats, and how those stats turn into HP.
