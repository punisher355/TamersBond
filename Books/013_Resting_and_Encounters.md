# **Chapter 13: Resting & Encounters**

## **What Is an Encounter?**

An encounter is a defined period of dramatic activity: a fight, a dangerous puzzle, a tense negotiation, or any situation where the GM calls for structured turns and meaningful stakes. Dark Digivolution ends at the end of each encounter. Conditional stat modifiers clear at the end of each encounter too.

## **Time**

Time doesn't move at the same rate in the Human World and the Digital World, and how the two line up is entirely up to the setting. Some campaigns run the two worlds on completely different clocks: weeks can pass in the Digital World while only minutes go by back home. Others keep the two worlds synced exactly, a minute spent in the Digital World is a minute spent in the Human World too. Neither approach is more correct than the other; it's a setting decision the GM makes for their own campaign, the same as anything else about how the two worlds relate.

The Digital World doesn't even have to be consistent with itself. Some settings have a Digital World where the sun never moves, stuck at the same point in the sky no matter how long anyone spends there. Others run the opposite way, locked into permanent night. The Digital World isn't bound to behave like a normal world at all, and a GM is free to have how time passes there shift from one hour to the next if that's what the setting calls for.

## **Resting and Adventuring**

As a baseline, a party should rest for 10 hours for every 14 hours they spend adventuring. Resting is where the party cooks, sleeps, and tends to their wounds.

When the party rests, both Tamer and Digimon restore their HP fully, and every Tamer's Hope refills to its max.

If a rest gets interrupted partway through, the party still gets that HP and Hope restoration, but they'll need to finish out the rest itself at the next opportunity they get.

Feeding both a Tamer and their Digimon during a rest takes 2 portions of food, one for each, to keep their Hope Pool at full strength.

### **Scavenging**

Once per rest, a Tamer may attempt a Field Ops check against DN 10 to scavenge for food. On a success, they find enough to feed themselves and their own Digimon for that rest, though not the rest of the party; any other Tamer who wants to feed their own pair this way needs to make their own attempt. Food found by scavenging can't be saved for later. Whatever isn't eaten during that same rest simply disappears.

If the party misses a rest, misses a meal, or both, their Hope Pool drops by the amount below:

| Missed Rests or Meals | Effect on Hope Pool |
| --- | --- |
| 0 (rested and fed) | Normal, no penalty |
| 1 | Hope pool halved |
| 2 | Hope pool quartered |
| 3 | Hope pool reduced to one eighth |
