# Chapter 1: The World

Before you create your Tamer or choose a Digimon partner, it helps to understand the world you're stepping into.

*Tamer's Bond* takes place across two worlds: the Human World and the Digital World. They're connected, but exactly how that connection works is up to the campaign you're playing.

## The Digital World

The Digital World exists alongside the Human World, formed from the enormous amount of data that flows through modern civilization. It's a place where the rules people are used to don't always apply.

The landscape can be beautiful, dangerous, or just plain weird. Mountains made from crystalized data can stretch across the horizon, oceans of code can change shape, and entire areas can look completely different from one day to the next.

The Digital World isn't meant to feel like a normal fantasy world with a different coat of paint. It's a living, changing place, and its inhabitants can have a real effect on the environment around them.

> **The Digital World Is Not Fixed**
>
> The Digital World can be changed by those who understand how it works.
>
> A powerful Digimon might tear apart a mountain range and reshape it into a single massive peak. A Tamer who has learned enough about the world's underlying code might find a way to open a passage between two distant locations.
>
> To someone from the Human World, this might look like magic. In the Digital World, it's simply part of how things work.
>
> This also means that the Digital World your group visits at the beginning of a campaign doesn't have to look the same by the end of it. The characters can leave their mark on the world, for better or worse.
>
> The GM decides what is possible within the campaign and how much the Digital World can be changed. The players decide what their characters do with that freedom.

## The Human World

The Human World is whatever you decide it is for your campaign. Your story might take place in a major city, a small town, or even a single neighborhood where strange things have started happening.

Unlike the Digital World, the Human World is generally stable. Buildings don't normally move overnight, streets stay where they were built, and reality tends to behave the way people expect it to.

How Digimon fit into human society is one of the first decisions the GM should make.

Maybe Digimon are already a normal part of everyday life. Maybe only a handful of people know they exist. Or maybe most people think Digimon are nothing more than rumors and strange stories on the internet.

There isn't one correct answer. Pick whatever works best for the kind of campaign you want to run.

| **Setting Question** | **What It Changes** |
| --- | --- |
| Do humans know Digimon exist? | This affects how Tamers and their partners are treated. A Digimon walking into a convenience store is very different when everyone already knows what Digimon are. |
| Can people travel between the two worlds freely? | This determines how often the campaign moves between the Human World and the Digital World. |
| Do governments and other authorities know about Tamers? | Tamers might be ignored, watched, recruited, or even treated as a threat. |
| How common are Digimon in everyday life? | This helps establish the tone of the campaign, from a world full of wonder and discovery to one where humans and Digimon simply live alongside each other. |

## Digimon

Digimon are digital lifeforms born from data. They can think, feel, form relationships, learn from their experiences, and change as they grow.

Most Digimon have some desire to become stronger, although what "stronger" means to them can be very different from one Digimon to another. Some want to become powerful fighters. Others want to protect their friends, reach a particular evolution, or simply find a place where they can live peacefully.

Evolution is one of the most important parts of a Digimon's life. A Digimon can grow into stronger forms as it gains experience and reaches the conditions necessary to evolve. When a Digimon is defeated, it may eventually return to a Digi-Egg and begin its journey again.

That cycle is a normal part of life in the Digital World.

What makes each Digimon interesting, however, is what they want beyond simply becoming stronger.

| **What a Digimon Wants** | **What That Looks Like in Play** |
| --- | --- |
| To become the strongest | The Digimon looks for powerful opponents and may challenge other Digimon simply because it wants to test itself. A challenge doesn't necessarily mean the Digimon is hostile. |
| To protect something or someone | The Digimon may be protective of a particular person, place, or group. It might be suspicious of strangers and become aggressive when what it protects is threatened. |
| To evolve into a specific form | The Digimon has a personal goal tied to its evolution. This could influence where it travels, who it works with, and even which risks it is willing to take. |
| To live peacefully | The Digimon has little interest in fighting. That doesn't mean it is helpless, though. When something it cares about is threatened, it may show a completely different side of itself. |
| To understand humans | The Digimon is curious about the Human World and the people who live there. This curiosity can eventually develop into a strong bond with its Tamer. |
| Something entirely its own | Not every Digimon needs to fit neatly into one of these examples. Work with your GM to create something that fits your character and the campaign. |
