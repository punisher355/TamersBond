# **Chapter 16: Items**

Items are objects Tamers and Digimon carry, equip, and use. Some are found as loot. Others are bought, and buying means dealing with one of two currencies.

## **Currency**

DigiDollars (DD) are the currency of the Digital World, what Digimon and Digital World merchants actually deal in. Real World Money is the ordinary money a Tamer already has access to back home, however they'd normally come by it. Some items only ever turn up for sale on one side of that divide and are priced in whatever currency that world uses. Others are available in both places and carry a separate price tag for each, since DD and Real World Money aren't pegged to any fixed exchange rate between them.

## **Item Types**

The Digivice sits apart from everything else in this chapter as the primary item every Tamer needs, and the other five types below round out the rest of a Tamer's gear.

| Type | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Digivice | The item every Tamer needs. Its only job is letting a Tamer's partner digivolve, but it has addon slots that can be filled with different Digivice addons drawn from different seasons. One equipped at a time. |
| Equipment | Worn by the Tamer, goggles, a helmet, a jacket, whatever the item actually is. Provides a permanent boost for as long as the Tamer has it on. One equipped at a time. |
| Accessory | Something a Tamer holds dear, whether it came from something that happened during the adventure or was given to them before it even started. One equipped at a time. |
| Gadget | Equipped rather than consumed. Carries a set number of uses that refresh at each rest, and spending one can hand over a new action or boost a skill check. One equipped at a time; can only be swapped when the Tamer takes a rest. |
| Supply | Used up the moment it's used. No equip limit; a Tamer can carry as many as they like. |
| Food | What a Tamer or their Digimon eats. Most food provides a boost to something lasting until their next rest. Only one bonus at a time. |

A Tamer can only have one of each equippable type active at once: one Digivice, one Equipment, one Accessory, and one Gadget. Food and Supply items are never equipped in the first place, so this limit doesn't touch them.

Every Tamer starts with a Digivice. It is the key item to your bond with your partner Digimon, and it is required to help them digivolve.

## **Equipment**

Equipment is something a Tamer can swap out whenever they rest, and whatever piece they're wearing adds its bonus for as long as they keep it on. The first five pieces below are all clothing.

| Equipment | Cost (REM) | Cost (DD) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold Weather Gear | 120 | 60 | When taking damage from a Wind attack, treat it as if you were Resistant to it. |
| Warm Weather Gear | 120 | 60 | When taking damage from a Fire attack, treat it as if you were Resistant to it. |
| Poncho | 120 | 60 | When taking damage from a Water attack, treat it as if you were Resistant to it. |
| Hiking Gear | 160 | 80 | Once between rests, when not in an encounter, make a Field Ops check against DN 10. If you pass, restore 2 Hope. |
| Vest | 200 | 100 | Once per encounter, you may subtract 10 damage from a hit. This can be declared after the damage is rolled. |

## **Accessory**

Accessories are the most personal item type in this chapter, things a Tamer holds onto because of what they mean, not because a shop stocked them. That doesn't stop them from carrying a real mechanical benefit alongside the sentiment. Almost all of them come from the Human World in the first place.

| Accessory | Cost (REM) | Cost (DD) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Photograph | 60 | 0 | Once between rests, using your Basic Action, look at it to steady yourself. Restore 1d8 Hope. |
| Lucky Charm | 80 | 0 | Once between rests, reroll one roll you just made and take the new result. |
| Friendship Bracelet | 60 | 0 | Permanent +1 to any Friendship skill check. |
| Worn Pocket Watch | 60 | 0 | Permanent +1 to any Reliability skill check. |
| Cellphone | 70 | 0 | Once between rests, look something up. Gain +2 on your next skill check tied to what you found. |

## **Gadget**

Gadgets carry a set number of uses instead of a single permanent effect, and every use spent comes back once the Tamer rests.

| Gadget | Uses (Refresh at Rest) | Cost (REM) | Cost (DD) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glasses | 3 | 60 | 120 | Spend one use to add 1d4 to a Radar check. |
| Flashlight | 4 | 45 | 90 | Spend one use in a dark environment to create a source of light, letting you see as though the space were lit normally. |
| Makeup Kit | 3 | 60 | 120 | Spend one use to add 1d4 to a Rally check. |
| Small Toy | 3 | 60 | 120 | Spend one use to add 1d4 to a Tame check. |

## **Supply**

Supply items are used up the moment they're used, no equip slot, no limit on how many a Tamer can carry at once. Using one normally costs a Basic Action, unless the item itself says otherwise.

| Supply | Cost (REM) | Cost (DD) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bandages | 20 | 20 | Restore 4 HP to one target. Can be used on a Tamer or a Digimon. |
| First Aid Kit | 50 | 50 | Restore 10 HP to one target and remove one status effect. Can only be used outside of combat. |
| Antidote | 40 | 40 | Remove all Poison from one target immediately. |
| Rubber Ball | 25 | 25 | Use before making your Basic Attack this turn. Treat the attack as though it had the [RANGE 4] tag. |
| Nicknacks | 25 | 25 | Use to immediately add one extra use to an equipped Gadget. |
| Energy Drink | 30 | 30 | Restore 2 Hope. |

## **Food**

Food is what a Tamer and their Digimon actually eat, the kind of thing that can satisfy the feeding requirement from Chapter 14. Every price below covers feeding both the Tamer and their Digimon together; buying enough for just one of the pair costs half the listed price.

| Food | Cost (REM) | Cost (DD) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Digi-Ration | 10 | 20 | A candy bar or power bar sold in both the Human World and the Digital World. Counts as a meal for both Tamer and Digimon. Prevents hunger. |
| Instant Noodles | 25 | 50 | Once, as a Free Action, restore 1d6 HP. |
| Cheeseburger | 25 | 50 | Once, as a Free Action, Add 1d4 damage to your next attack. |
| Onigiri | 25 | 50 | Once, as a Free Action, restore 1d6 Hope. |
