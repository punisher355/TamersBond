## **DNA Digivolution**

DNA Digivolution is a special form of Digivolution in which two partner Digimon combine into a single, more powerful Digimon. Unlike normal Digivolution, DNA Digivolution is a temporary transformation that follows its own rules.

### **DNA Digivolution Requirements**

To DNA Digivolve:

* Two willing Tamers must have their partner Digimon active on the battlefield.
* Both Digimon must meet the requirements of the DNA form.
* At least one of the two Tamers must have the DNA form unlocked.
* Both Digimon must be at the stage required by the DNA form.

The exact stage required to DNA Digivolve depends on the DNA form being used.

### **Initiating DNA Digivolution**

DNA Digivolution is an exception to the normal Digivolution rules.

The DNA transformation **does not count as one of the two Digivolutions a Digimon can normally make during an encounter**, and it does not count against the normal limit of being two stages above Default Stage.

When the requirements are met, both partner Digimon combine and are replaced by their DNA form.

The DNA form remains active for **6 turns**.

DNA Digivolution costs **no Hope** to initiate and requires **no Hope to maintain**.

### **DNA Crest Stats**

The DNA form uses its own **Species Base** for each Crest Stat. The **Tamer Rank** and **Digimon Invested** layers come from the two partners.

For each Crest Stat:

* Use the higher **Tamer Rank** of the two Tamers.
* Use the higher **Digimon Invested** value of the two partner Digimon.
* Add the DNA form's **Species Base**.
* Apply any **Conditional** modifiers normally.

The two partners' full Crest Stat totals are **not** added together, and the DNA form does not use either partner's Species Base.

**Example:** One Tamer has Courage 4 and their Digimon has 3 ranks of Digimon Invested Courage. The other Tamer has Courage 2 and their Digimon has 5 ranks of Digimon Invested Courage. The DNA form has a Species Base Courage of 6.

The DNA form's Courage is:

**Species Base 6 + higher Tamer Rank 4 + higher Digimon Invested 5 = 15 Courage.**

### **DNA HP**

The DNA form has its own HP and Max HP.

When DNA Digivolution ends, the DNA form's remaining HP is divided between the two returning Digimon as evenly as possible. Neither Digimon can return with more HP than their own Max HP.

For example, if the DNA form has 19 HP remaining when the fusion ends, one partner returns with 10 HP and the other returns with 9 HP.

### **DNA Move Pool**

A DNA Digimon can draw on the techniques of both partners. It has its own **Signature Move**, along with the **Selected Moves of both partner Digimon**.

When DNA Digivolution begins, the DNA Digimon gains access to:

* Its own DNA Signature Move.
* The 3 Selected Moves of the first partner.
* The 3 Selected Moves of the second partner.

This gives the DNA Digimon up to **6 Selected Moves** in addition to its Signature Move.

All Selected Moves still follow the normal stage requirements from Chapter 11. A DNA Digimon can only use a move if its current Stage is equal to or higher than the Stage at which that move was learned.

When DNA Digivolution ends, each partner returns with their own Move Pool and Selected Moves unchanged.

If the DNA form has a Signature Move that the partners have not previously unlocked, that Signature Move is added to **both partners' Move Pools** when the DNA Digivolution ends.

### **Ending DNA Digivolution**

DNA Digivolution automatically ends after **6 turns**.

The Tamers may also end the DNA Digivolution early as a Free Action.

If the DNA form reaches **0 HP**, the DNA Digivolution ends immediately.

When DNA Digivolution ends, both partner Digimon return at the **Current Stage they had before DNA Digivolution began**.

The DNA form's status effects are cleared when the transformation ends.

If the DNA form reaches 0 HP, the normal **0 HP: The Cascade** rules apply to the returning Digimon.

### **Special Digivolution Exception**

DNA Digivolution does not follow the normal two-stage Digivolution limit.

A DNA form may therefore reach a Stage that would normally be impossible to reach through the Digimon's two available Digivolutions alone.

The two partner Digimon must still meet the DNA form's listed requirements before the transformation can occur.
