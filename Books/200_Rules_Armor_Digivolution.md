# **About This Section**

This section contains expanded rules introduced by the A New Destiny module. These mechanics are not part of the core rulebook; they only apply when the GM has enabled this module for their campaign.

## **Armor Digivolution**

Armor Digivolution is an ancient, pseudo-evolutionary process that predates the modern digivolution chain. Rather than climbing through stages by spending Hope, a Digimon draws power directly from a Digi-Egg, a relic tied to that egg's element. The result is not a true evolution. It is a separate kind of power entirely, borrowed rather than earned.

### **Requirements**

The Tamer must have the matching Digi-Egg item in their possession, along with the D-3 Digivice Addon in order to use it to Armor Digivolve. The Digimon must be a species that can reach that specific Armor form. The Armor form does not need to be one of the Digimon's own unlocked Digivolution forms; a compatible species can use it regardless of which forms are currently unlocked.

### **How It Works**

There is no Hope cost to initiate Armor Digivolution and none to maintain it, since the Digi-Egg powers the form instead. The Armor form lasts for 6 turns from the moment it is activated, after which it expires automatically and the Digimon reverts to their previous stage. Armor Digivolving does not count towards the 2 stage limit.

### **Digivolving From an Armor Form**

A Digimon in an Armor form can continue digivolving normally by spending Hope. When the Digimon digivolves past the Armor form the Armor form ends immediately.

### **Expiring Naturally**

When the 6 turn limit is reached the Armor form expires. The Digimon reverts to whichever stage they were at before Armor Digivolution. Hope costs resume normally on the Tamer's next turn.

### **The Digi-Egg Item**

Each egg is tied to one specific Armor form and is never consumed when used. It can be used once per encounter.

### **Unlocking Armor Forms**

Finding a Digi-Egg automatically unlocks the corresponding Armor form for a compatible Digimon. No EXP is required. No additional purchase is needed. The egg is the unlock.
