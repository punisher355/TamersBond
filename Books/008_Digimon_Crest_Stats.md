# Chapter 8: Digimon Crest Stats

Digimon use the same six Crest Stats as Tamers: Courage, Friendship, Love, Knowledge, Sincerity, and Reliability. What each one governs in combat is identical to what's described in Chapter 5; Courage still governs Hit Rate, Friendship still governs Speed, and so on down the line. What's different is how a Digimon's number in each stat actually gets built.

## The Four Layer System

A Tamer's Crest Stat is a single number they raise directly. A Digimon's Crest Stat is four numbers stacked on top of each other:

> **The Four Layers**
>
> **Species Base.** Set by the Digimon's species and current evolution stage. This is baked into the line you chose in Chapter 7, and it changes automatically every time your Digimon digivolves or dedigivolves.
>
> **Tamer Rank.** Your own rank in that same Crest Stat, added straight across. This is the literal mechanical expression of the bond: raise your own Courage, and your partner's Courage rises with it, at no extra cost to either of you.
>
> **Digimon Invested.** EXP your Digimon has spent from their own pool to raise that stat directly. This is the one layer that never resets, not on digivolution, not on dedigivolution, not ever.
>
> **Conditional.** Temporary buffs and debuffs from the current fight. Cleared at the end of every encounter.

Add all four together and that's the number that actually shows up in combat. Take Agumon's Courage as an example: Species Base 4, his Tamer's Courage rank 3, and 2 more EXP-ranks his Tamer has spent directly on Agumon's own Courage. With no active Conditional modifiers, his total Courage is 4 + 3 + 2 + 0, or 9.

That total, not any single layer of it, is what gets used everywhere a Digimon's Crest Stat matters, including the Hit Formula in Chapter 10.

## Digimon HP

Your Digimon's Max HP is built from their total Sincerity, the same four-layer total described above:

**Digimon Max HP = 20 + (total Sincerity × 4)**

For example, a Digimon with a total Sincerity of 8 across all four layers has a Max HP of 20 + (8 × 4), or 52.

## Digimon EXP Pool

Covered in Chapter 7: your Digimon's EXP pool always mirrors the total EXP your Tamer has earned, spent completely separately. Everything below assumes you're spending out of that pool.

There are exactly two things a Digimon's own EXP can buy: raising their Crest Stats directly (the Digimon Invested layer, right below), and unlocking additional Digivolution forms (further down this chapter). Skills work differently for a Digimon than they do for a Tamer; see Digimon Skills at the end of this chapter for why.

## Raising Digimon Stats

Species Base and Tamer Rank take care of themselves; Digimon Invested is the layer you buy up directly, and each rank costs more than the last:

| Upgrade | EXP Cost |
| --- | --- |
| 0 → 1 | 100 EXP |
| 1 → 2 | 200 EXP |
| 2 → 3 | 300 EXP |
| 3 → 4 | 400 EXP |
| And so on | Rank × 100 EXP per step |

## Digivolution Form Unlocks

Reaching a new stage doesn't automatically hand you every Digimon that stage could become. Your Digimon's own EXP pool gates how many alternate forms at each stage you can actually unlock:

| Stage | Min Digimon EXP | First Form | Each Additional Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rookie | Free | Free | 50 EXP per form |
| Champion | 2,000 EXP | Free | 100 EXP per form |
| Ultimate | 8,000 EXP | Free | 300 EXP per form |
| Mega | 20,000 EXP | Free | 500 EXP per form |

> **Digivolution Path Rule**
>
> A Digimon can only digivolve into a form its current stage actually leads to. Owning an unlocked form through some other path doesn't help if there's no valid line connecting your Digimon's current form to it. If Agumon has Garurumon unlocked through an alternate path, he still can't digivolve into Garurumon, because Agumon's own line simply doesn't go there. The path has to be valid from your current form, not just unlocked somewhere in your pool.

## Digimon Skills

Digimon don't buy their own skill ranks the way Tamers do. When a Digimon needs to make a skill check, whether that's a Tame check to bond with a wild Digital creature, a Trace check to follow a scent, or an Archive check to recall something about the Digital World, they roll their Tamer's rank in that same skill, not a rank of their own.

If a Tamer's Trace is rank 3, their Digimon's Trace checks also roll 3d6 and get summed against the DN exactly like the Tamer's own would; Chapter 6 covers the full mechanic. Raise your own skills, and your partner's competence at the same things rises right alongside you, the same way your Crest Stats already feed their combat math above.

This is why a Digimon's own EXP pool never goes toward skills at all. Everything they can spend on themselves goes to exactly two things, both covered above: raising their Crest Stats directly through the Digimon Invested layer, and unlocking additional Digivolution forms at each stage. There's no separate skill list or skill-purchase table for a Digimon to work through here; their skills are whatever their Tamer's sheet already says.

With a Digimon's stats, HP, and skills all settled, Chapter 11: Attacks & Tags covers the last piece of their sheet: the moves they actually fight with.
