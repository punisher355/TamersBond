# Chapter 6: Crest Stats & Hope

Every Tamer has six Crest Stats. Each one is built from three layers, your Primary Crest Bonus, your Level-Up Points, and any Conditional modifiers from the current fight, added together for a Total (Chapter 4 covers picking your Primary Crest and buying Level-Up Points, including the full EXP cost table). Whenever this chapter refers to a Crest Stat's value, it means that Total.

Crest Stats aren't just yours, either. Your Digimon partner uses the same six stats, and their total in each one is built partly from your Level-Up Points in it (the "Four Layer System" in Chapter 9). Raise your own Courage, and your partner hits harder in the same fight. That's the core relationship this whole game runs on, so before you spend a single point, it helps to know exactly what each stat is doing on both sides of the table.

## The Six Crest Stats

### Courage (CRG)

Courage is what it looks like when someone acts under pressure instead of freezing. In combat, it governs Hit Rate: both you and your Digimon roll 1d20 + Courage against a target's Reliability + 10 to land an attack (full sequence in Chapter 11). It's also the stat that determines how hard a [PUSH]-tagged move shoves someone around.

Courage governs five skills: Blitz, Ironclad, Crusher, Ghost, and Roar.

### Friendship (FRD)

Friendship measures how well someone reads others and moves with them. Mechanically, it governs Speed, setting both movement and turn order for you and your Digimon alike. Digimon move a number of spaces equal to their Friendship and act in initiative equal to that same number. Tamers move and act at double that rate, Friendship × 2 spaces and Friendship × 2 initiative. A Tamer with Friendship rank 3 moves 6 spaces and acts on initiative 6; their Digimon moves 3 spaces and acts on initiative 3. Ties in turn order are broken by whoever has the higher raw Friendship stat.

Friendship governs three skills: Scan, Rally, and Broadcast.

### Love (LVE)

Love is awareness and protectiveness, noticing what's about to happen to someone before it happens. In combat, it governs Damage Reduction: subtract your total Love from every hit of incoming damage.

Love governs three skills: Mend, Radar, and Tame.

### Knowledge (KNW)

Knowledge is tactics, technical understanding, and reading how the Digital World's underlying logic actually works. It's the primary source of Damage, added directly to the PR dice roll of every attack you or your Digimon land.

Knowledge governs more skills than any other stat, seven in total: Decode, Jack In, Modify, Trace, Archive, Command, and Playback. That breadth is deliberate. Knowledge Tamers tend to be the ones holding the party's information together outside of combat as much as inside it.

### Sincerity (SNC)

Sincerity is a character's core resistance, what they're actually made of underneath everything else. It's the stat behind HP for both halves of the pair: your Digimon's Max HP is built from total Sincerity (Chapter 9), and your own HP as a Tamer is derived from your Sincerity rank directly (see below). It also governs how well a Sleep status effect can be resisted, through the Firewall skill, and how well Freeze, Paralyze, and Confuse can be resisted, through the Core Drive skill.

Sincerity governs three skills: Firewall, Reinforce, and Core Drive.

### Reliability (RLB)

Reliability is preparation and protection, the stat behind being hard to catch off guard. In combat, it sets the Miss Threshold: an attacker has to roll 1d20 + their Courage and meet or beat your Reliability + 10, or the attack simply doesn't land. It's the mirror image of Courage in the hit formula, and it's also the backbone of a lot of the party's actual healing and field survival.

Reliability governs three skills: Zero Error, Field Ops, and Recovery.

> **What "Highest Crest Stat" Means**
>
> Not every playable Crest lines up with one of the six Crest Stats above. Hope, Light, Darkness, and a handful of other season-specific Crests are personal or story Crests rather than mechanical ones, they don't have their own rank to raise or their own line on your sheet.
>
> When a class ability tied to one of these non-mechanical Crests lists a requirement like "Requires: Highest Crest Stat 4+," it means exactly what it says: look at your own six Crest Stats (Courage, Friendship, Love, Knowledge, Sincerity, Reliability), find each one's Total across all three layers, and use whichever stat's Total is currently highest on your Tamer's sheet. If two or more stats are tied for highest, you meet the requirement, use whichever tied stat you like for anything that also asks you to make a check with it. This always refers to the Tamer's own six stats, never the partner Digimon's four-layer totals.

## Hope

Hope isn't one of the six Crest Stats, and it isn't tied to any of them either. It tracks your Tamer's overall growth: specifically, the total amount of EXP they've earned over their whole career, starting with their initial 1,500.

It doesn't matter where that EXP ends up. Whether you pour everything into one Crest Stat, spread it across Skills, or sink it deep into a Class tree, every point you've ever earned counts toward your Hope Pool the same way.

> **Hope Doesn't Cost You Anything to Earn**
>
> Your Hope Pool grows automatically as your total EXP earned goes up. You never spend EXP on Hope directly, and holding EXP in reserve instead of spending it doesn't shrink your Hope either. It's about what you've earned over your career, not what's currently sitting unspent.

Use the table below to find your Hope Pool. If your total EXP falls between two entries, use the value from the most recent milestone you've reached, not an average between them.

| Total EXP Earned | Hope Pool |
| --- | --- |
| 1,500 | 20 |
| 2,000 | 25 |
| 3,000 | 35 |
| 4,000 | 40 |
| 5,000 | 45 |
| 7,500 | 55 |
| 10,000 | 65 |
| 15,000 | 80 |
| 20,000 | 100 |
| 30,000 | 125 |
| 40,000 | 145 |
| 50,000 | 165 |
| 60,000 | 185 |
| 70,000 and up | 200 |

For example, a Tamer with 3,150 total EXP has passed the 3,000 milestone, so their Hope Pool is 35, even though they haven't reached 4,000 yet.

Hope is what keeps your Digimon digivolved. It's spent automatically at the start of your turn in combat to cover the Hope cost of their current stage. If it ever hits 0, your partner immediately reverts to their Default Stage before they can act (full breakdown in Chapter 13: Digivolution, including what happens if you choose to push past what you can actually afford). It refills completely between sessions and after a long rest, so nobody starts a new session running on empty.

Tying Hope to total EXP earned means every path through character growth grows your Hope at the same rate, whether that path is stat-heavy, skill-heavy, class-heavy, or a mix of all three.

### Maximum Hope

A Tamer reaches maximum Hope at 70,000 total EXP earned, giving them a Hope Pool of 200. Reaching that milestone doesn't mean every Crest Stat, Skill, or Class is maxed out too, just that Hope itself has topped out.

## Tamer HP

Your own HP is derived from your Sincerity Total, the same three-layer total used everywhere else on your sheet: **HP = 12 + (Sincerity Total × 4)**.

## Temporary HP

Some effects grant **Temporary HP**: a separate pool layered on top of your normal HP. This applies to both Tamers and Digimon.

- **No cap.** Temporary HP has no maximum. If something grants you more while you still have some, it stacks on top of whatever you currently have.
- **Absorbs damage first.** Any damage you take is subtracted from Temporary HP before it touches your actual HP. Once Temporary HP hits 0, further damage starts coming out of your real HP as normal.
- **Resets on rest.** Temporary HP drops to 0 when you rest, whether or not it was used up. It doesn't carry over.
