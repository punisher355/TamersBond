# Chapter 2: Overview

In *Tamer's Bond*, each player controls two characters: a Tamer and their Digimon partner.

Both characters can take part in combat, although Digimon are usually the ones doing most of the fighting. That doesn't mean Tamers are helpless. What your Tamer does, how you spend Hope, and how you develop your partner can all have a big impact on the outcome of a battle.

The two characters are meant to work together. Your Tamer's abilities directly affect your Digimon, so building one is also part of building the other.

## What You Need to Play

| **Players** | **One GM and 1–4 players** |
| --- | --- |
| Dice | d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100 |
| Character Sheets | One for each Tamer and Digimon |
| Grid Map | Used for combat. 1 square = 1 space. |
| Tokens | Miniatures or tokens for all participants |

## The Core Relationship

Every Tamer has six Crest Stats. These stats are more than just numbers on the Tamer's sheet, they directly affect their Digimon partner.

When your Tamer grows stronger, their Digimon grows with them.

Each Crest Stat is tied to a specific part of your Digimon's abilities. Some affect combat directly, while others determine things like movement, health, or how difficult the Digimon is to hit.

| **Tamer Crest Stat** | **Feeds Into Digimon** | **What It Does in Combat** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courage | Courage (Hit Rate) | Adds to attack rolls when determining whether an attack hits. Roll 1d20 + Courage against the target's Reliability + 10. |  |
| Friendship | Friendship (Speed) | Determines movement and turn order. | Digimon move a number of spaces equal to their Friendship. Tamers move Friendship × 2 spaces. Digimon turn order = Friendship. Tamer turn order = Friendship × 2. |
| Love | Love (Damage Reduction) | Reduces the damage taken from incoming attacks. | Subtract Love from incoming damage on every hit. |
| Knowledge | Knowledge (Damage) | Adds to the damage dealt by attacks. | Knowledge is the primary damage bonus for attacks. |
| Sincerity | Sincerity (HP) | Determines the Digimon's total Hit Points. |  |
| Reliability | Reliability (Miss Threshold) | Determines how difficult the Digimon is to hit. | An attacker must meet or beat Reliability + 10 to successfully hit. |
| Hope (derived) | Digivolution Resource | Provides the resource used to digivolve. | Hope is not a combat stat. See the Hope Pool table for more information. |
