# Data Squad: Burst Digivolution

This section contains expanded rules introduced by the Data Squad module. These mechanics are not part of the core rulebook, they only apply when the GM has enabled this module for their campaign.

## **Burst Digivolution**

Burst Digivolution isn't something a Tamer activates. Where Armor, Biomerge, and Spirit Digivolution are all things a Tamer reaches for on purpose, spending an item, a merge, or a relic to power a partner up, Burst Digivolution is the Digimon proving something on its own, in the middle of a fight, without anyone choosing to trigger it.

It costs no Hope to trigger and no Hope to maintain. It requires the Digivice Burst addon and no Digivolving Action. It rides entirely on the back of the Digimon's own combat rolls, and once it starts, it only ever gets stronger the longer the fight keeps going well.

### **Triggering a Burst**

Whenever a Digimon lands a natural 20 (a crit, per Chapter 10) on an attack roll, roll a d100 against the number needed for its Current Stage:

| **Current Stage** | **Roll Needed (d100, at or under)** |
| --- | --- |
| Rookie | 10 |
| Champion | 20 |
| Ultimate | 35 |
| Mega | 60 |

Fresh and In-Training Digimon cannot trigger a Burst. Mega II uses the same number as Mega, the same as it does everywhere else in the rules.

Roll at or under the listed number and the Digimon starts Bursting immediately.

### **While Bursting**

Once a Digimon is Bursting, every natural 20 it lands afterward adds another stack automatically. No further trigger roll is needed once Bursting has already started, only the very first crit has to earn it.

Each stack grants:

- **+1 to Hit Roll**
- **+1 to Damage Roll**
- **+1 Damage Reduction** against incoming hits

These are flat bonuses applied directly to the combat math, and they stack with no upper limit for as long as Bursting continues.

Hope costs are completely unaffected. The Digimon still pays its normal Hope Cost Per Turn for whatever stage it's currently holding, exactly as Chapter 12 already requires. Burst Digivolution is layered entirely on top of that, it changes nothing about how digivolving itself works or what it costs.

### **Losing a Burst**

A Digimon can still digivolve, dedigivolve, or sidedigivolve freely while Bursting, nothing about Burst Digivolution blocks it. Doing so just costs them the Burst.

Whenever a digivolution of any kind happens while Bursting, whether that's a Tamer choosing to digivolve or dedigivolve, a forced digivolution succeeding, the Cascade dropping the Digimon's stage at 0 HP, or a Dark Digivolution taking hold, Bursting ends immediately and every stack it had built up is lost. There is no partial loss and no way to protect the stacks once a stage change happens.

Bursting and all of its stacks also end the moment the encounter itself ends, the same as any other Conditional effect.

| **This Rule Is Opt-In** Burst Digivolution is a Season 5 module, independent of Armor Digivolution, Biomerge, and Spirit Digivolution. A campaign can run any combination of these, or none at all, they don't interact with each other unless the GM specifically says otherwise. |
