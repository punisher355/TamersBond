# Spirit Tamer

*An optional rule for players who want to become the Digimon.*

Normally a Tamer fights alongside a partner Digimon. The Spirit Tamer rule replaces that partner entirely, the Tamer becomes the Digimon themselves. This is a self-contained opt-in system. Other players at the table are completely unaffected. A GM can enable Spirit Tamer for one player without changing anything about how the rest of the game works.

## The Rules

A Spirit Tamer has no partner Digimon. Instead, the Tamer is the Digimon. They have a single turn in combat, there is no separate Tamer turn and no separate Tamer action economy.

A Spirit Tamer builds one Move Pool, exactly like a partner Digimon: a Signature Move per stage reached, the Basic Attack, and three Selected Moves refreshed at every long rest. They digivolve, maintain Hope, and follow all standard digivolution rules unless stated otherwise below.

### Digivolving

When a Spirit Tamer digivolves, they themselves become the Digimon. While in that form, all normal Digimon rules apply to them exactly as they would to a partner Digimon at that stage, including HP, stats, moves, the Cascade, and Dark Digivolution.

### Forms

A Spirit Tamer has exactly one Fresh form, one In-Training form, and one Rookie form. They may never gain additional forms at these stages.

Champion and higher forms are unlocked using the Digimon EXP thresholds from Chapter 8's Digivolution Form Unlocks, the same rules as a standard partner Digimon.

### EXP

A Spirit Tamer's EXP pool is still divided between Tamer and Digimon as normal, even though it's one physical form. The Tamer-half EXP still buys Crest Stats, Skills, and Classes; the Digimon-half EXP still buys Digimon Invested stat ranks and Digivolution Form Unlocks. Both pools track the same total EXP earned, spent completely separately, the same as any Tamer-and-partner pair.

### Default Stage

A Spirit Tamer's Default Stage is always Rookie. It can never be changed, raised, or affected by any rule, ability, item, or story exception. Daily Rest Recovery does not apply to it. Their Max Default Stage is always Rookie as well, and there is no way to adjust that either.

### Shared Move Pool

The Tamer half and Digimon half of a Spirit Tamer share a single Move Pool. At Rookie stage, the Tamer half may use any move already in the pool, and the Digimon half may use any move the Tamer half has added to it. Any bonus that would normally buff a Tamer's Basic Attack also buffs a Spirit Tamer's Basic Attack, and any bonus that would normally buff a Digimon's Basic Attack does too, it works in both directions.

## Spirit Digivolution

Spirit Digivolution is an ancient power that predates the modern digivolution chain, channeled through a Spirit, a fragment of one of the ten Legendary Warriors bound into a physical item. Rather than climbing through stages by spending Hope, a Digimon draws power directly from the Spirit itself. The result is not a true evolution. It is a separate kind of power entirely, borrowed rather than earned.

### Requirements

The Tamer must have the matching Spirit (or Spirits, for forms that draw on more than one) in their possession, along with their Digivice, in order to use it to Spirit Digivolve. The Digimon must be a species that can reach that specific Spirit form. The Spirit form does not need to be one of the Digimon's own unlocked Digivolution forms, a compatible species can use it regardless of which forms are currently unlocked.

### How It Works

There is no Hope cost to initiate Spirit Digivolution and none to maintain it, since the Spirit powers the form instead. The Spirit form lasts for 6 turns from the moment it is activated, after which it expires automatically and the Digimon reverts to their previous stage. Spirit Digivolving does not count towards the 2 stage limit.

### Digivolving From a Spirit Form

A Digimon in a Spirit form can continue digivolving normally by spending Hope. When the Digimon digivolves past the Spirit form the Spirit form ends immediately.

### Expiring Naturally

When the 6 turn limit is reached the Spirit form expires. The Digimon reverts to whichever stage they were at before Spirit Digivolution. Hope costs resume normally on the Tamer's next turn.

### The Spirit Item

Each Spirit is tied to one specific Spirit form and is never consumed when used. It can be used once per encounter. Some Spirit forms draw on more than one Spirit at once (see The Spirits below); every required Spirit must be in the Tamer's possession to activate that form, but each individual Spirit still only needs to be used once per encounter to power its part of the fusion.

### Unlocking Spirit Forms

Finding a Spirit automatically unlocks the corresponding Spirit form for a compatible Digimon. No EXP is required. No additional purchase is needed. The Spirit is the unlock.
