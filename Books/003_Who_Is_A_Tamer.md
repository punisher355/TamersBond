# Chapter 3: Who Is a Tamer?

A Tamer is someone who has formed a real bond with a Digimon. Not ownership, and not partnership in the loose sense of two people who just happen to work together. What a Tamer believes, wants, and is willing to risk shapes what their partner becomes, both in the fiction and at the table. Before you touch a single number, this chapter is about putting an actual person on the page.

Every player builds one of these. By the end of Chapter 6 you'll have a complete Tamer, and Chapter 7 is where you build the Digimon standing next to them.

> **Starting EXP Budget**
>
> Every new Tamer begins with 1,500 EXP to spend during creation. All six Crest Stats and all 24 skills start at rank 1 for free; that EXP is what takes them beyond the baseline, and you'll spend it across this chapter and the two after it.
>
> Your Digimon partner gets the same deal. They start with their own 1,500 EXP, spent separately in Chapter 7. Nothing you spend on yourself comes out of their pool, and nothing they spend comes out of yours.

## Who a Tamer Interacts With

**Their Digimon.** This is the relationship the whole game is built around. Your Crest Stats feed directly into your partner's combat stats, your Hope keeps them digivolved, and your choices about how hard to push them matter as much as anything they roll. Chapter 5 covers exactly how that link works, stat by stat.

**Other Tamers.** Most groups run with one to four players, each with their own Tamer and partner. You're not just fighting alongside each other. A Tamer's skills and class abilities regularly exist to support the party as a whole, not just their own Digimon.

**Everyone else.** NPCs, rival Tamers, wild Digimon, the world the GM is running. A Tamer's skills are what let them navigate all of that without a Digimon doing the talking: reading a lie, calming someone down, following a trail. That's the ground Chapter 6 covers.

## Building Your Tamer

Start with who they are before you touch a single number. The Digital World tends to respond to conviction as much as it responds to raw strength, and a Tamer's want, their fear, and the flaw that's actually going to cause problems all shape the story as much as anything on the stat line.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | What do people call you? |
| Age | Most Tamers are 10 to 14. No hard rule. |
| Gender & Pronouns | How do you identify? |
| Appearance | What do people notice first? |
| Personality | Two or three words that sum you up. |
| Background | Where are you from? What was life like before? |
| Want | What does your character want most deeply at their core? |
| Fear | What do they fear most? Be specific. |
| Flaw | One honest weakness that will actually cause problems. |
| Crest | Which of the six Crest Stats defines you? |

Take the Crest question seriously. It's more than flavor: your Crest is almost always the stat you'll raise first and fastest, which means it's also the stat most likely to set the ceiling for how far you can push your favorite skills (Chapter 6 covers exactly how). Picking a Crest means picking the trait your Tamer leans on when things get hard, and every class tree in every season book is built around one.

Once you know who your Tamer is, the next step is picking what they can actually do beyond raw stats and skills. That's Chapter 4: Classes, and once that's settled, Chapter 5: Crest Stats & Hope is where the actual numbers start.
