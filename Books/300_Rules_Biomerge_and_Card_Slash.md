# A New Destiny: Biomerge & Card Slash

## **Biomerge Digivolution**

Biomerge Digivolution is the merging of a Tamer's body and consciousness with their Digimon partner. The result isn't a Tamer riding inside a giant monster. It is one being made of both of them, with a single will and a power that neither could reach alone.

Biomerge lets a Tamer jump straight to Mega without spending Hope, ignoring the two-stages-above-Default limit.

### **Requirements**

To Biomerge, the Digimon must have its Mega form unlocked (20,000+ Digimon EXP). The Tamer must have their Digivice with them, as with any other Digivolving Action, and must have the Biomerge Accessory equipped.

Any Tamer who meets these requirements can Biomerge. Biomerge can only be used once per rest period.

### **Initiating Biomerge: The Corruption Check**

Biomerge is a Digivolving Action. It uses the same resource as a normal Digivolve and costs no Hope. Before the merge resolves, the Tamer must make a Corruption Check.

**The Corruption Check:** Choose one of the following skills: Firewall, Core Drive, Reinforce, or Ironclad. You can choose a different skill each time you Biomerge. Roll against DN 12.

**On a success, Clean Biomerge:** The Tamer and Digimon merge into a single form at Mega stage. The merge lasts 6 turns.

**On a failure, Corrupted Biomerge:** The form that emerges is corrupted. The Tamer is still inside, off the field under the same rules as a Clean Biomerge, but the form itself follows the standard corruption rules from Chapter 8 of the Core Rulebook.

The Tamer cannot voluntarily end the Biomerge while the form is corrupted, and it does not run on a timer. It remains until the encounter ends or the form reaches 0 HP.

### **While Merged**

The Tamer is removed from the battlefield. They cannot be targeted, use items, or act independently.

The Biomerge form acts on the Tamer's original turn in initiative order. The Digimon's normal turn slot becomes inactive, so the merged form does not act twice.

The merged form uses the Digimon's full Mega stats normally. No Hope is spent to initiate or maintain the Biomerge.

A Clean Biomerge lasts for 6 turns unless it is ended early or the encounter ends first. A Corrupted Biomerge has no timer and lasts until the encounter ends or the form reaches 0 HP.

The Tamer's purchased class abilities remain available through the merged form and can be used on its turn as normal.

The merged form is locked at Mega stage. Once Biomerge is initiated, no further digivolution of any kind, upward or downward, is possible for the rest of the encounter.

### **Ending Biomerge**

Biomerge ends when one of the following occurs:

1. A Clean Biomerge's 6-turn duration expires.
2. The Tamer spends a Digivolving Action on their turn to revert early. This can only be done with a Clean Biomerge.
3. The merged form is reduced to 0 HP.
4. The encounter ends.

**If Biomerge ends for any reason other than the merged form reaching 0 HP:** The merged form's remaining HP is split between the Tamer and Digimon however the player chooses. The split does not have to be even, but neither character can receive more HP than their individual maximum HP.

The Digimon reverts to Default Stage, and the Tamer returns to the battlefield in the space the merged form occupied.

**If the merged form is reduced to 0 HP:** Both the Tamer and Digimon are removed from the encounter. They are treated as Defeated according to Chapter 9 and are out of actions for the rest of the encounter.

## **Card Slash System**

Tamers do not just watch their Digimon fight. They can intervene directly. Modify Cards are physical cards embedded with Digital World data that, when slashed through a Digivice, alter the parameters of their partner in real time.

Any Tamer can use a card as their Basic Action as long as they have the D-Power Digivice Add-On.

### **Acquiring Cards**

Cards can be purchased, found, traded, or awarded by the GM as loot.

Tamers can carry any number of cards since they are small and light, but each card is single-use. Once a card is slashed, it is spent and cannot be used again.

Some rare cards cannot be purchased. The GM determines how these cards are obtained.

### **Using a Card: The Slash**

Slashing a card costs the Tamer's Basic Action on their turn, and only one card can be slashed per turn.

The Tamer must be holding the card and have their D-Power Digivice Add-On equipped. The card's effect applies immediately.

Cards cannot be slashed on another character's turn unless a class ability or card effect specifically allows it.

### **Card Rarity**
