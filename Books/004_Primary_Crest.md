# Chapter 4: Picking Your Primary Crest

Your Primary Crest is the Crest you chose in Chapter 3, and it does more than describe who your Tamer is. It sets your starting Crest Stats before you spend a single point of EXP.

## The Three Layers

Every Tamer's Crest Stat is built from three layers added together:

> **Tamer Crest Stat = Primary Crest Bonus + Level-Up Points + Conditional**
>
> **Primary Crest Bonus.** A fixed starting spread, determined entirely by your Primary Crest. You don't choose how these points are distributed; the Crest you picked decides it. This layer never changes once chosen and costs no EXP.
>
> **Level-Up Points.** Ranks you buy with EXP, spent on whichever stats you want, in whatever order you want. Every stat begins at Level-Up rank 0.
>
> **Conditional.** Temporary buffs and debuffs from the current fight. Cleared at the end of every encounter.

All six Crest Stats begin at 0 in every layer except whatever your Primary Crest Bonus provides.

## Courage Primary Crest, Starting Spread

A Tamer whose Primary Crest is Courage starts with this Primary Crest Bonus spread:

| Stat | Primary Crest Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Courage (CRG) | 2 |
| Friendship (FRD) | 1 |
| Love (LVE) | 1 |
| Knowledge (KNW) | 1 |
| Sincerity (SNC) | 1 |
| Reliability (RLB) | 1 |

This spread is fixed. A Courage Primary Crest always looks exactly like this table; it isn't arranged by the player.

## Buying Level-Up Points

Level-Up Points start at 0 in every stat and climb independently of your Primary Crest Bonus. The first rank in any stat's Level-Up track costs 100 EXP, no matter which stat it is:

| Upgrade | EXP Cost | Running Total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 → 1 | 100 | 100 |
| 1 → 2 | 200 | 300 |
| 2 → 3 | 300 | 600 |
| 3 → 4 | 400 | 1,000 |
| 4 → 5 | 500 | 1,500 |
| 5 → 6 | 600 | 2,100 |
| 6 → 7 | 700 | 2,800 |
| 7 → 8 | 800 | 3,600 |
| 8 → 9 | 900 | 4,500 |
| 9 → 10 | 1,000 | 5,500 |

**Worked example.** A Courage Primary Crest Tamer has a Courage Bonus of 2, for free. Spending 100 EXP buys Level-Up rank 1 in Courage, making their Courage total 2 (Bonus) + 1 (Level-Up), or 3. Spending another 200 EXP buys Level-Up rank 2, bringing Courage to 4 total. The Crest Bonus layer stays at 2 the whole time; only the Level-Up layer grows.
